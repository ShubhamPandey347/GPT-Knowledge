# Mbank vs Banky — UAE Account Research

**Research date:** 2026-08-26  
**Context:** User has AED 5,000+ salary, does not want salary transfer, wants AED 0 maintenance/minimum-balance banking, debit card and digital-wallet support, and preferably a cheque book.

> **Important:** This document records the state of the research and the user's observed app onboarding screen. Banking fees and product terms can change. The live app/account-opening terms should be treated as the final source before opening an account.

## Key Finding

Mbank and Banky are **not two unrelated banks**. Banky is a newer digital-wallet/everyday-banking product powered by **Al Maryah Community Bank (Mbank)**. Mbank is the underlying UAE bank; Banky is a newer product/brand experience from the same bank.

## What the user observed in the Mbank mobile app

On the account-opening page, the user reported seeing:

- **Banky — Free**
- **Mbank — AED 26.25–210 monthly fee range**

This current app experience conflicts with the older Mbank package information previously used in the analysis. Therefore, do **not** recommend opening a paid Mbank package based only on the older KFS without checking the current in-app terms.

## Mbank — relevant characteristics from the earlier documented research

The earlier research identified the following for the Mbank Gold Current Account based on an older published Key Facts Statement:

- Gold Current Account: previously documented as having no monthly service fee
- No minimum balance
- Current-account cheque-book facility
- First cheque book previously documented as free
- Subsequent cheque leaves/books previously documented as paid
- Debit card and virtual-card functionality were previously documented
- Apple Pay / Google Pay support was previously documented for the virtual debit card

**Caution:** These details came from older material and should be re-verified against the current 2026 account terms/app before relying on them.

## Banky — relevant characteristics

Current product positioning identified during the research:

- Digital wallet / everyday-banking experience from Al Maryah Community Bank
- No minimum balance
- Free/no monthly account-maintenance fee advertised for the relevant product
- IBAN
- Everyday transfers/payments/remittances
- Jaywan debit card
- Intended for balances up to approximately AED 20,000 according to the current product positioning

The current Banky information reviewed did **not establish a cheque-book facility**. Therefore, do not assume Banky provides cheque books.

The earlier research also noted that Banky's current fee information had physical/virtual card features marked as coming soon at that time. This should be rechecked in the live app because card availability and digital-wallet support may have changed.

## Practical Recommendation

Given the user's priorities and the **current app showing Banky as free while Mbank packages display AED 26.25–210**, the safest approach is:

1. **Do not open a paid Mbank package yet.**
2. Treat **Banky** as the leading candidate for a free everyday account if its current terms satisfy the user's needs.
3. If a **cheque book is mandatory**, investigate the current Mbank current-account offering separately and confirm the exact fee/package in the app before accepting terms.
4. Verify the current Banky card's **Apple Pay and Google Pay** support before opening if mobile-wallet support is important.
5. Use the live in-app Key Facts Statement / fees and terms immediately before account opening as the final authority.
